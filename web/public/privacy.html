Instanter

Privacy policy

Last updated 25 August 2026.

Instanter is a deadline-triage tool for eviction-defence clinics. This policy covers the website at this domain and the Instanter mobile app.

What is collected about you

Nothing. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising identifiers, no tracking pixels, and no third-party trackers. Nothing you do here is associated with you as a person, because nothing here knows who you are.

What the app sends

The app and the website call one public endpoint on this domain to run a triage sweep over a fixed, synthetic demonstration dataset, and to record whether you chose to approve or defer that sweep. The request contains no personal information: it carries a capacity number and, when you answer, the word you chose.

What is stored

  • The outcome of a demonstration run, keyed by a random identifier, so the page can show you the result. It expires automatically within seven days.
  • A per-day count of how many runs have been started, so a public endpoint that calls a paid model cannot be run without limit. It is a number, not a list of people.
  • Ordinary server request logs kept by Amazon Web Services, which hosts this service.

The case records are not real people

Every case record shown in Instanter is synthetic and labelled as such in the data itself. No tenant, landlord, attorney, or court record belonging to a real person is present. The statute, the court calendar, and every computation are real; the people are not.

Who it is shared with

Nobody. Nothing is sold, rented, or shared. The service runs on Amazon Web Services, which processes requests on our behalf as an infrastructure provider.

Children

Instanter is a professional tool for legal-aid staff and is not directed at children.

Legal information, not legal advice

Instanter states operative facts and computes statutory deadlines for review by a licensed attorney. It does not provide legal advice and it is not a substitute for a lawyer.
